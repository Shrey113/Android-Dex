Android Phone Setup (Android 9.0 – 15+)

01
Unlock Developer Options

Go to Settings → About Phone and tap Build Number 7 times rapidly.

02
Enable USB Debugging

In Settings → Developer Options, toggle USB Debugging ON. (Xiaomi/MIUI: also toggle "USB debugging (Security settings)" ON).

03
Connect Cable & Allow RSA Prompt

Connect your phone via USB cable (File Transfer / MTP mode). When the prompt "Allow USB debugging?" appears on your phone, check Always allow and tap Allow.

04
Automatic Desktop Connection

Launch Android DEX on your computer. It connects in less than a second and streams your apps in resizable floating windows!

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Android DEX require root or bootloader unlock?
No root is required. Android DEX operates completely through standard official Android Debug Bridge (ADB) protocols. It does not void your warranty or trip Knox/SafetyNet.
Will mobile games detect this as an emulator?
No. The game runs 100% on your physical phone hardware. Input is sent as native touch events. Anti-cheat systems recognize your genuine physical smartphone without emulator bans.
Why does macOS say "Android Dex is damaged"?
macOS Gatekeeper attaches a quarantine flag to unsigned binaries. Run xattr -cr /Applications/Android\ Dex.app in Terminal to remove the attribute instantly.
Is my data private and offline?
100% offline and local. Zero servers, zero telemetry pings, and zero analytics. Everything stays strictly between your PC and phone.
