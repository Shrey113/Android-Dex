Quick Start

Up & Running
in Minutes

No complex drivers. No additional installs. ADB is bundled and ready.

STEP 01

Enable Developer Options

Open Settings → About Phone on your Android and tap Build Number seven times until Developer Options unlock.

STEP 02

Turn On USB Debugging

Navigate to Settings → Developer Options and toggle USB Debugging or Wireless Debugging to ON.

STEP 03

Launch Android DEX

Extract the downloaded zip and run the app. It auto-detects your device and boots the full desktop experience automatically.
